# The Constitution of Eleventh Thesis [ELEVE]

*Version 1, adopted 30 September 2026 (YC128). From now on, changes are made only under Article XII. Amendments 1.1 (Article VII.4–VII.6) and 1.2 (Article IV.4) were published to members (corp bulletin) and the public (channel MOTD) on 6 October 2026. The comment period under Article XII.2 ends on 13 October 2026, when the CEO decides.*

## Preamble

Capsuleers have long interpreted New Eden, charting its markets, its wars and its dead. The point is to change it.

Eleventh Thesis is an industrial collective. The cluster usually separates those who work from those who own. This collective binds the two together. Whoever contributes to the common product holds a claim on it, and that claim is public, recorded and paid. This constitution sets out how.

---

## Article I: Name, purpose and seat

1. The collective is **Eleventh Thesis**, ticker **ELEVE**.
2. Its purpose is to turn its members' labour into a shared stock of capital through collective production, and to return the surplus to those who produced it.
3. Its **headquarters** is Nonni III – Caldari Funds Unlimited Depository, where the ledger and the shares are kept. Its **workshop** is Nonni VI – Kaalakiota Corporation Factory, where production happens.

## Article II: Membership

1. Any capsuleer who accepts this constitution may apply.
2. New members serve a **probation of fourteen days**. During probation a member:
   - holds no corporation roles beyond what every member gets;
   - may contribute, and every contribution is recorded in the ledger from the first day;
   - is issued the shares earned during probation when probation ends.
3. Applicants may be asked to authorize the collective's ESI tool for a basic background check. Nothing beyond what that check needs is requested.
4. Any member may leave at any time. Article XI says what happens to their claims.

## Article III: Contribution and the ledger

1. **What counts as a contribution.** At launch, the collective accepts:
   - high-sec ore;
   - minerals;
   - planetary goods of tiers P1 and P2.

   The directors may add other categories, such as salvage or mission loot, by announcing them.
2. **How to contribute.** Contributions are made by **item-exchange contract to the corporation** at the Nonni VI workshop. The contract is the record.
3. **Valuation.** Each contribution is valued at the **Jita 4-4 buy price on the day of delivery**. The ledger records the price used.
4. **The ledger is open.** Every member may read the full ledger: contributions, sales, costs, the reserve and the share register.
5. **Founders contribute under the same rules.** The founders' own work, whether mining, missions, planetary production or industry, is recorded and rewarded exactly as any other member's.

## Article IV: Payment for contributions

1. **Consignment phase (now).** The collective sells or processes contributed goods. The contributor is paid **90% of the price the goods actually sold for, net of fees, when the sale happens**. The ledger shows when each lot is sold and paid.
2. **Instant-payment phase (later).** Once the reserve reaches **500 million ISK**, the collective switches to paying **90% of the Jita buy price at delivery**. This switch is announced at least seven days in advance.
3. The 10% retained, together with any value added by the collective's production, makes up the **surplus** (Article VI).
4. **Production inputs.** Contributed goods that the collective uses in production are never sold as delivered. During the consignment phase their contributor is paid **90% of their value at delivery (Article III.3) when the product they went into is sold**. Where a product is sold in parts, payment follows in the same proportion. The value added by production, or any loss on it, falls to the collective under Article IV.3.

## Article V: Shares

1. A member earns **one share for every 10,000,000 ISK** of contributed value, as valued under Article III.3.
2. **Earned shares** are the only shares that receive dividends.
3. **The founding block.** The 1,000 shares created when the corporation was founded are held by the founders. They exist to secure the collective's control during the founding period (Article VIII). **They carry votes but no dividends.**
4. Shares are recorded in the ledger. Where EVE's share mechanics allow it, earned shares are also transferred in game.

## Article VI: Surplus and dividends

1. **Surplus** means sales revenue, minus payments to contributors, minus production costs, minus fees and taxes.
2. Each month the surplus is divided as follows:

   | Share | Fund | Purpose |
   |---|---|---|
   | 40% | Reserve | Payment liquidity and operating safety |
   | 20% | Reinvestment | Blueprints, research, ships issued to members |
   | 40% | Dividend pool | Paid to holders of earned shares |

3. **Dividend per earned share = dividend pool ÷ total earned shares outstanding.** Dividends are paid monthly from the dividend wallet, with a published statement.
4. A month with no surplus pays no dividend. The statement still shows why.

## Article VII: Founder capital

1. The founder's starting capital is an **interest-free loan** to the collective, recorded in the ledger.
2. It is repaid **only from the reserve**, and only once the reserve exceeds the liquidity that paying contributors requires.
3. Founder capital **earns no shares and no dividends**. Capital doesn't earn here; labour does.
4. **Founder means of production.** Planetary colonies, ships and other assets a founder owns remain the founder's property. Their output is contributed under Article III and paid under Article IV like any member's: the founder earns shares and payment for the work, and nothing further for owning the means.
5. **Lending payments back.** A founder may lend any part of a payment received under Article IV back to the collective. Such a loan falls under Articles VII.1–VII.3: it is interest-free, earns no shares and no dividends, and is repaid only from the reserve.
6. **Liquidity threshold.** Founder loans held as cash count towards the 500 million ISK at which instant payment begins (Article IV.2). The ledger shows reserve, founder loans and undistributed surplus separately, so members can see how much of that liquidity is owed back to the founder.

## Article VIII: Governance and control

1. The collective is run by its CEO and directors.
2. **Founding period.** Until the dilution trigger below is reached, the founders keep more than half of all votes. This protects a small collective against a takeover by vote. The protection is stated openly here, and it is temporary.
3. **Dilution trigger.** The founding period ends on whichever comes first:
   - **ten active non-founder members**, where active means having contributed in the last thirty days; or
   - **29 March 2027**, six months after founding.
4. **When the trigger is reached:**
   - the founders reduce their voting share below half by distributing founding shares to members in proportion to their earned shares;
   - a corporation vote then ratifies this constitution or amends it.

## Article IX: Taxation

1. The corporation tax on members' ISK and LP income is **0%**.
2. Any change to it is announced at least fourteen days in advance and explained in terms of the ledger. After the founding period, a change also needs a corporation vote.

## Article X: Security and roles

1. Roles are granted only as far as a member's work requires them, and never during probation.
2. Corp manufacturing roles (Factory Manager, Rent Factory Facility) are granted only to members who have finished probation and whose work needs them.
3. The collective owns no structures, so it can't be the target of a war declaration. Anchoring a structure, which would change that, needs the members' consent: a corporation vote after the founding period, or an announced consultation before it.
4. Theft from the collective, or sabotage of it, ends membership and forfeits unissued shares. The ledger records the reason.

## Article XI: Departure

1. Shares a member has earned remain their property when they leave.
2. The collective asks departing members to sell their earned shares back at **book value**: the collective's net assets (reserve, plus stock at Jita buy, minus liabilities) divided by total earned shares. Selling back is requested, not compelled.
3. Contributions still being sold on consignment are paid out to the departed member when they sell, as normal.

## Article XII: Amendment

1. Any member may propose an amendment in the corporation channel or by in-game mail to the CEO.
2. **During the founding period**, the CEO decides after a comment period of at least seven days, and publishes the reasons.
3. **After the founding period**, amendments need a corporation vote.
